Stress is a natural part of life, especially during exams or high-pressure situations. However, it can be managed effectively by adopting healthy habits.

1. Practice deep breathing exercises to calm your nervous system.
2. Maintain a consistent sleep schedule to improve cognitive performance.
3. Engage in regular physical activity, such as walking, yoga, or cycling.
4. Eat balanced meals and avoid excessive caffeine or sugar.
5. Use time management tools, such as planners or digital calendars, to reduce feelings of overwhelm.
6. Talk to a friend, teacher, or mental health professional when needed.

These habits can help reduce stress levels and improve overall mental health.
